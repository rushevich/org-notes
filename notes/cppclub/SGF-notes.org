* Fundamental Info

C++ club is _non-sponsored_ - we do not have a UF faculty advisor, and operate more or less separately from the university.

** Eligibility for Funding

We are a registered organization, and hence we need to register with the Department of Community & Belonging (CoBe).

*NOT* allowed to collect fees/dues.

** Key Terms

- Docutraq :: Online system for student orgs to request and use SG funding
- Budget Requests :: Itemized summary / estimate of organizations expected costs for upcoming semesters
- SAR :: student activity request , for reviewing and approving access to general funds (not travel related)
- TAR :: SAR but for travel related
