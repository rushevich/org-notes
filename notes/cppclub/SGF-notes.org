* Fundamental Info

C++ club is _non-sponsored_ - we do not have a UF faculty advisor, and operate more or less separately from the university.

** Eligibility for Funding

We are a registered organization, and hence we need to register with the Department of Community & Belonging (CoBe).

*NOT* allowed to collect fees/dues.

** Budget Types
- Base :: Can be used for tech, GBMs, advertising, events
- Operational :: Approved by Budget and Appropriations Committee -- GBMs, food for GBMs, awards, and non-event related advertising
- Event :: All events excluding GBMs or tabling. up to $20k per budgeted semester
- Travel :: Budget for expenses related to travel beyond UF’s campus

** Budgeted Semesters
Two budgeted semesters, defined by when the *activity starts*:
- Summer B / Fall :: activities starting July 1 - December 31
- Spring / Summer A :: activities starting January 1 - June 30

Budget request windows are scheduled months ahead of each start date so the committees have time to review and approve. Plan backwards from this, not from the event date.

** SAR Descriptions

One description line must contain the following:
- Where will event be held, and what time?
- How many people are expected to attend (if using food line)
- Must be descriptive if purchasing items like supplies, food, etc.
- Presidents / treasurers should be requesting this info if missing

** Reimbursements
Student turning in the receiupts will be the vendor listed on the SAR/TAR and must submit all the receipts at one time
Receipts must be itemized with proof of purchase and submitted within 30 days from the date listed on the SAR/TAR

Preferred option: scan and email receipts in pdf/jpg format to: sgfdocuments@sg.ufl.edu with subject line ~Receipts for SR (or TAR) ###~

See also [[*Travel]] for the travel-specific version of this rule.

* Budget Lines

Spending is governed by the 808 codes, which are strict about both what you buy and how you buy it. Current balances for every line live in Docutraq.

Available lines: Programs, Food, Advertising, Honorariums, Copies, Awards, Travel, Base Funding.

*Code 808.8 lists all funding limits.* Check it before writing a request.

** Advertising
Covers social media ads, banners, flyers, clothing, promo items, websites, and similar.

- *Never use copyrighted images.* SG funds cannot be used to pay fines levied against you for usage.
- Items must be listed *separately by quantity and unit price* on the SAR. This is the most common reason requests get bounced.
- Promo items: $3.00 per unit maximum. Setup and design charges count toward that cap; freight and taxes do not.
- Promotional clothing (shirts, hats, totes, caps): $18 per unit.
- Clothing bought with SG funds *cannot be sold*, so no selling club shirts we funded this way.

** Programs
Covers venue rental, decorations, supplies, and similar.

- $500 cap on decorations per event, $400 cap on wet labs.
- Equipment can be requested under Base Funding. Otherwise it must be listed ahead of time on an approved Event Budget Request.
- SAR items must be itemized. Writing "supplies" is not enough.
- Code 810.2 #12 lists which Programs items the Operational Budget will fund.
- Code 808.7: off campus recreational activities (as defined in 800.28) are only half funded under the Programming line when an on-campus equivalent or similar alternative exists. B&A decides during review.

** Food
- *Must state expected number of attendees* and exactly what is being purchased, in the SAR description.
- $7.00 per student maximum.
- No Coke products. UF has a Pepsi contract, so Coke purchases cannot be reimbursed.
- Delivered on-campus catering must use a UF approved caterer: [[https://www.bsd.ufl.edu/catering]]
- Palm & Pine holds exclusive rights at certain locations: [[https://businessservices.ufl.edu/services/catering/classic-fare-exclusive-catering-locations/]]
- In the Reitz Union, any UF approved caterer can be used if attendance is 40 or fewer.

** Honorariums
Any individual or group providing a service on UF campus, paid a stipend agreed on by both parties to cover expenses. Examples: performers, speakers, photographers. Payment can include their fee plus travel expenses.

- Students are *not* to pay honorarium expenses out of pocket.
- Up to $5,000 per budgeted semester, requested on an Event Budget Request.
- Caps: DJs $200, photographers $750.
- *Base Funding cannot be used for honorariums.*

** Copies
- Use the SG copy center, room 2365 of the Reitz Union. No SAR needed.
- Contact the Copy Center any time for the current balance.
- *Not available under Base Funding.*

** Awards
- $40 maximum per award.
- Must be competitive based.

** Base Funding
Used for technology, general body meetings, advertising, or events. Cannot cover honorariums, and copies are not available under it.
